**Pest Report Pro** is a mobile web app that lets pest control technicians generate professional PDF reports directly on their phone — no laptop, no subscription, no internet required.

**What you get:**
- ✅ Service Report (FREE) — Document pest findings, treatment methods, chemicals used, safety precautions, and collect client signatures
- ✅ Quote Generator (Pro) — Create itemized professional quotes with your company branding
- ✅ Chemical Log Export (Pro) — Compile all chemical application records into compliance-ready PDFs
- ✅ Your company logo & technician signature automatically added to every PDF
- ✅ Works completely offline once installed to your home screen

**Perfect for:**
Solo technicians and small pest control businesses who need to create professional reports in the field without expensive software.

**After purchase:**
Your license key will be emailed to you. Enter it in the app to unlock all Pro features instantly.

**Access the app:**
https://jar0401.github.io/pest-report-pro/

**Install on your phone:**
- iPhone: Open in Safari → Share → "Add to Home Screen"
- Android: Open in Chrome → Menu → "Add to Home Screen"
