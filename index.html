Questions? Answered

Do I need an internet connection? +

Nope. Pest Report Pro is a PWA (Progressive Web App). Once installed, it works completely offline. All your data is stored locally on your device.

Is my data secure? +

Yes. Everything is stored locally on your phone or computer. No cloud servers, no data breaches, no one else can access your client information.

Can I add my company logo? +

Absolutely. Go to Settings, upload your logo, add your license number, and set your technician signature. Every PDF will be branded with your company info.

What devices does it work on? +

iPhone, Android, iPad, tablet, desktop — any device with a modern web browser. Install it to your home screen for the best experience.
